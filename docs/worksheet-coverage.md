> `values-priorities`, `pros-cons-skills`, `reduce-vulnerability`,

> `paired-muscle`, `body-scan`, `mindfulness-events`. The tables below still show
> them as `Missing`; treat this banner as authoritative and re-sync the rows when
> convenient. See `devin-handoff-worksheets.md` for what is left.

Compare against *DBT Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition*
(Linehan, Guilford 2015). Book page numbers below are the printed page numbers,
not PDF page numbers.

The app ships one form per **worksheet family**. Where the book has lettered
variants (`1a`, `1b`, …) that differ only in space or in being a worked example,
a single form covers the family. `Missing` therefore means "no form of any kind
covers this exercise", not "one lettered variant is absent".

## Highest-value gaps

Ordered by how much the exercise is used in a standard 24-week skills group:

1. Emotion Regulation Worksheet 9 — Steps for Reducing Vulnerability (the weekly
   "what did you do for ABC PLEASE this week" sheet; `please-tracker` only covers
   the PLEASE half).
2. Distress Tolerance Worksheet 8 — Reality Acceptance Skills (the weekly log for
   radical acceptance, turning the mind, willingness).
3. Mindfulness Worksheets 2, 2a, 2b, 2c — Core Skills Practice / Calendar.
4. Interpersonal Effectiveness Worksheet 5 — Tracking IE Skills Use.
5. Emotion Regulation Worksheet 1 and General Worksheet 1 — the two "pros and
   cons of doing this at all" sheets, used at the start of every module.
6. Emotion Regulation Worksheet 16 — Troubleshooting Emotion Regulation.
7. Distress Tolerance Worksheets 17, 18 and Worksheet 13 — the addiction block.
8. The calendar variants (Mindfulness 8, 9; Distress Tolerance 4a, 4b, 6c) — a
   low-effort reuse of the existing calendar pattern.
